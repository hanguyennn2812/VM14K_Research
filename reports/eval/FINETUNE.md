# VM14K: LoRA fine-tuning a 2B model on the train split

Generated by `scripts/training/analyze_finetune.py` from runs written by `scripts/training/sft_mcq_lora.py`. Model: Qwen/Qwen3.5-2B, LoRA r=16 on every attention, Gated DeltaNet and MLP projection (15.6M trainable parameters, 0.82%), one epoch over the 7,313 usable train rows, batch 16, lr 2e-4, Intel Arc 140T iGPU. Scoring reads the next-token logits of the option letters after the paper's Fig. 6 prompt (thinking off), so no answer goes unparsed; compare these runs with each other, not with the generation-scored runs in `SUMMARY.md`. Test split: 1,658 questions, never used for training or tuning.

## 1b. Where the gain comes from

The zero-shot model prefers some letters regardless of content. Dividing each letter's probability by its mean over the val split (no labels used, no training) removes that preference. Mean zero-shot probability per letter on val: 2 options A 67% / B 33%; 4 options A 31% / B 37% / C 17% / D 15%.

| comparison (A → B) | n | A % | B % | B − A | only A right | only B right | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| zero-shot → letter-prior corrected | 1658 | 41.8 | 46.3 | +4.5 | 141 | 215 | 0.0001 * |
| letter-prior corrected → LoRA shuffled | 1658 | 46.3 | 51.0 | +4.7 | 180 | 258 | 0.00023 * |

Letter-prior corrected run picks A on 28.5% of items.
