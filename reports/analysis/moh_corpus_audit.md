- **4 documents are assembled from more than one `partNN` file** (2201-20131a21, 2557-0e31e588, 2558-42570d8b, 2834-e82db33c). Split by filename and one part goes to train while another goes to test — the same document, so the retriever is tested on text it trained on.

Separately, **15 documents are a single part with the rest missing from disk** (mostly a lone `part02`). That is not a leakage problem but a grounding one: a question written on a `part02` page may depend on definitions that live in the absent `part01`. Either exclude these 15 documents from question writing or state that questions are written only from pages that are self-contained.
