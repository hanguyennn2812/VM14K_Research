# MoH Guideline Corpus — Ingestion Specification (v1)

**Status:** draft, pre-freeze
**Scope:** Ministry of Health (BYT/SYT) clinical guideline PDFs, one zip, 27 specialty folders.
**Purpose:** turn the raw PDF dump into a frozen, auditable text corpus for training use, with every token traceable to a source document and every exclusion logged with a reason. Same discipline as the VM14K frozen artifact.

This is a spec, not results. Numbers below are **measured**, not estimated, and are regenerated by `scripts/analysis/moh_corpus_audit.py`. Full output: `reports/analysis/moh_corpus_audit.md`. Rerun the script rather than editing figures here by hand.

## 2. Extractability split

**Two filters are needed, not one.** Classify per page *and* per document — either alone admits material that should be excluded.

- **Per page** (< 50 non-ws chars): catches unreadable pages scattered *inside* otherwise good documents — cover scans, stamped approval pages, photographed tables. 65 such pages sit inside documents that are otherwise fine. A document-level rule alone would either discard those documents wholesale or silently admit their blank pages.
- **Per document** (< 50 chars/page averaged): catches whole scanned documents. Necessary because of a trap — see below.

**The digital-signature trap.** A scanned PDF signed with a government certificate carries one page of signature metadata (`Ký bởi: BỘ Y TẾ / Cơ quan: BỘ Y TẾ / Ngày ký…`), roughly 60–90 characters. That page clears the per-page floor. So a naive `usable_pages > 0` document filter calls a 136-page scan "born-digital" on the strength of one page containing no clinical content. **4 of the 5 scanned documents in this corpus fail exactly this way.** The document-level average catches all of them, and the separation is clean:

| doc | Specialty | Pages | Chars | Chars/page |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `3792` cervical cancer | Ung bướu | 77 | 0 | 0.0 |
| `636` first-aid | Cấp cứu - Sơ cứu | 136 | 68 | 0.5 |
| `2246` child health check | Nhi khoa | 93 | 68 | 0.7 |
| `2674` HIV testing | HIV | 70 | 58 | 0.8 |
| `3594` nutrition | Dinh dưỡng | 2 | 70 | 35.0 |
| *lowest genuine document* | — | — | — | **52.0** |

Resulting counts, on unique pages:

| Class | Pages | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible for use — text layer, in a surviving document | 6,972 | 94.0% |
| No text layer (< 50 chars) | 439 | 5.9% |
| Inside a document dropped as scanned | 378 | 5.1% |

(The last two overlap: 374 pages are both.) **100 of 105 documents survive.** Nearly all extractable *content* is in the eligible class, so the corpus is viable without OCR.

Per-page classification: `data/interim/moh_corpus_pages.jsonl` — one row per page with `nonws_chars`, `has_text_layer`, and `eligible` (the field page selection should filter on). Per-document rollup: `data/interim/moh_corpus_manifest.jsonl`.

Worst-affected specialties (usable/total pages): `Cấp cứu - Sơ cứu` **0/136**; `HIV` 132/207; `Ung bướu` 214/293; `Nhi khoa` 648/740.

## 3. Known data issues to handle before use

### 3.1 Content duplicate — FACT
One byte-identical pair (md5 `664c9e95…`):
- `Da liễu/…4416…benh_Da_lieu.pdf`
- `Truyền nhiễm/…4416…benh_Da_lieu.pdf`

Content is dermatology guidance, correctly homed in `Da liễu`, misfiled a second time into `Truyền nhiễm`. **Action:** keep the `Da liễu` copy, drop the `Truyền nhiễm` copy, record the drop. No other md5 collisions in the set — single dedup, not a pattern.

Dedup rule: **content hash, not filename or path.** A folder-walk corpus builder would otherwise train on this twice and, if QA pairs are ever derived, produce cross-specialty label conflicts on identical content.

### 3.2 Multi-part documents — FACT (revised, was understated)

19 parent documents carry `partNN` files, 23 part files in total. They split into two very different cases, and the earlier "four documents, none exceeds 2 parts, no gaps" reading was wrong on both counts.

**(a) Genuinely multi-part — 4 documents, all parts present:**

| Doc | Parts on disk |
|---|---|
| `2834` HIV new-infection testing | 01, 02 |
| `2557` fundus photography / diabetic retinopathy | 01, 02 |
| `2201` Marburg haemorrhagic fever | 01, 02 |
| `2558` diabetic retinopathy diagnosis & management | **01, 06** — parts 02–05 missing |

`2558` has a gap: the filenames jump from `part01` to `part06`. Concatenating them produces text that reads continuously but is not.

**(b) Orphan fragments — 15 documents, one part present, the rest absent.** Mostly a lone `part02` with no `part01` (`3172`, `3874`, `5544`, `1242`, `1832`, `1921`, `2248`, `2341`, `2769`, `3133`, `40`, `5480`, `678`), plus a lone `part01` (`1856`, `4922`). These are not whole documents. A page in a `part02` can depend on definitions, abbreviations or dosing tables that live in the missing `part01`.

**Action:**
1. Map each part → parent document via the part-stripped filename stem; concatenate in part order; treat the result as one corpus unit with one `doc_id`. Do not tokenize parts independently.
2. `doc_id` — not filename — is the **train/test split key**. Splitting on filename puts `2557_part01` in train and `2557_part02` in test, which tests the retriever on a document it trained on.
3. Flag all 16 documents with an incomplete part run (the 15 orphans plus `2558`) as `incomplete_part_run` / `orphan_part`. Decide before question generation whether they are in scope; if they are, questions must be written only from self-contained pages.

Current per-document flags are in `data/interim/moh_corpus_manifest.jsonl`.

### 3.3 Mangled short-names — OPEN
Several files carry Windows 8.3 short-names (`QU25D4~1.PDF`, `QU30C0~1.PDF`, `QU54DF~1.PDF`). The underlying files are intact; only the display name is mangled. **Action:** resolve each to its real title (open + read header, or match by md5/size against the index) before it goes in the manifest. Don't freeze a manifest with unresolved names.

### 3.4 Cross-filing — NOTE
The `4416` case shows a document can be filed under more than one specialty folder. The manifest must allow a document to carry **multiple specialty tags** rather than assuming folder = single label. Deduplicate the *content*; preserve *all* folder memberships as tags.

## 4. Scanned files — exclusion policy

**Decision: exclude scanned files from v1. Do not OCR for v1.**

Rationale: OCR of Vietnamese medical text — dosages, drug names, tables — introduces silent numeric/character corruption. Feeding that into training unverified repeats the exact failure this project exists to catch in VM14K. A wrong digit in a dosage is worse than an absent document.

Rules:
1. Any **page** below the floor of **< 50 non-whitespace chars/page** is tagged `no_text_layer` with the measured value. Separately, any **document** averaging below the same floor is `scanned_dropped` — *not* "a document whose every page falls below it", which the digital-signature stamp page defeats (§2). A page is usable only if it clears the floor **and** its document survives.
2. Every drop is logged with reason — nothing silently disappears (same rule as VM14K's `quarantine_all.jsonl`).
3. **Coverage check:** if excluding scanned pages leaves any specialty with **zero** usable documents, flag that specialty to the supervisor as a coverage gap. Do not paper over it with bad OCR.
4. OCR is a possible **v2** sub-project with its own verification step, out of scope here.
5. **Always quote the floor and the extractor next to any page count.** Both change the answer, so a bare count is not reproducible.

**The floor is a choice, and the curve is flat where it sits.** Measured on unique pages:

| Floor | Pages below |
|---|---|
| 0 chars (no text at all) | 387 |
| < 10 | 409 |
| < 20 | 423 |
| < 30 | 428 |
| **< 50 (current)** | **439** |
| < 80 | 707 |
| < 100 | 729 |

Between 0 and 50 the count moves by only 52 pages, so the exact cutoff barely matters — good, the classification is not sensitive to a defensible choice of floor. It jumps sharply after 50 (439 → 707 by 80), which is the boundary where real but sparse pages — section dividers, short tables — start being swept in. **Do not raise the floor above 50 without inspecting what it removes.**

**Coverage gap found — supervisor decision needed.** `Cấp cứu - Sơ cứu` (Emergency Medicine) holds exactly one document, `636`, and it is a scan: 68 characters across 136 pages. The specialty has **zero usable documents**. This is precisely the rule-3 trigger. Emergency Medicine cannot support question generation from this corpus, and any claim of specialty coverage must either exclude it or state the gap outright.

Usable pages by specialty range from **0** (`Cấp cứu - Sơ cứu`) to **1,196** (`Truyền nhiễm`) — a spread that makes "sample evenly across the specialties" impossible as literally stated. Whatever sampling rule is used, state it and report the resulting per-specialty distribution. Full table: `reports/analysis/moh_corpus_audit.md` §4.

## 5. Corpus manifest — the deliverable

One row **per source document** (not per file). Format: JSONL + a TSV summary.

Per-document fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `doc_id` | stable id (e.g. decision number `2557`, or hash prefix if ambiguous) |
| `title` | resolved human title (short-names expanded) |
| `parent_files[]` | the one-or-more PDF files composing this doc (parts) |
| `specialty_tags[]` | all folders it appears under (≥1) |
| `pages` | total page count across parts |
| `chars` | extractable non-whitespace char count |
| `class` | `born_digital` \| `scanned_dropped` |
| `content_md5` | hash for dedup / provenance |
| `drop_reason` | populated iff excluded |
| `notes` | short-name resolution, misfiling, etc. |

Plus a **per-specialty coverage summary**: doc count, born-digital count, dropped count, total usable chars — so a coverage gap is visible at a glance.

**Freeze criteria** (all must hold before the corpus is declared v1):
- File denominator reconciled (§1 OPEN closed).
- All short-names resolved (§3.3 closed).
- Content dedup applied, drop logged (§3.1). — *done, `cross_filed_duplicate` flag*
- Parts concatenated per parent (§3.2). — *done, `parts_present` + `orphan_part` / `incomplete_part_run` flags*
- Every **page** classified with a reason, and every document rolled up to `born_digital` or `scanned_dropped`. — *done, per-page in `moh_corpus_pages.jsonl`*
- Exact word/token count recomputed on the final born-digital set (replaces the ÷5 estimate).
- Manifest stamped with build date + a hash of the manifest itself.

**Current state:** a working manifest is generated by `scripts/analysis/moh_corpus_audit.py` at `data/interim/moh_corpus_manifest.jsonl` (105 documents), alongside `data/interim/moh_corpus_pages.jsonl` (7,889 page rows). It carries `doc_id`, `specialty_tags[]`, `source_files[]`, `duplicate_paths[]`, `parts_present`, `content_md5[]`, `pages`, `usable_pages`, `chars_per_page`, `class`, `drop_reason` and `flags`.

The page file accounts for every one of the 7,889 page rows: 6,972 `eligible`, and each of the rest carries an `ineligible_reason` — 474 a second on-disk copy of the duplicate, 439 below the per-page floor, 4 signature-stamp pages inside dropped documents. It is in `interim/`, **not frozen** — the freeze criteria above are not all met. Missing before freeze: resolved titles (§3.3), the token count, and the manifest self-hash.

## 6. Intended use — decide before generating anything

Two distinct jobs, different formats, **not yet chosen** — flag to supervisor:

- **(A) Continued pretraining / domain adaptation** on raw guideline text. Uses the corpus as-is. Lower verification burden (no new labels created).
- **(B) Supervised finetune on QA/MCQ pairs** derived from the guidelines. Requires an LLM to generate questions from guideline text → **creates a second unverified dataset** needing the same doctor verification as VM14K. Do not start (B) quietly.

Pin this decision before any generation. If (B), it inherits the full VM14K verification pipeline, not a shortcut.

---

## 7. Provenance & integration

When MoH text is eventually mixed into training:
- Every training row stamped with `doc_id` + `content_md5` + corpus manifest hash.
- Dataset version (VM14K clean version + MoH corpus version) is a **config parameter, never hardcoded** — so a "VM14K only" vs "VM14K + MoH" ablation is clean and no data shifts silently between runs.
- MoH corpus freeze is independent of the VM14K prof-side blockers; it can be finalised now.
